Light check

How to use

  1. Download light-logger.hex
  2. Plug in a micro:bit V2. A MICROBIT drive should appear.
  3. Drag the hex file onto that drive and wait for it to finish.
  4. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  5. Press Log and pick the micro:bit serial port.
  6. Press Stop, then Save for a CSV or image.
